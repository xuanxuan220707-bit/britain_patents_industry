# britain_patents_industry
The project studies British sealed patents, United Kingdom coal production, and annual new railway mileage during 1802–1902. It examines the 1852 patent reform, correlations across historical indicators, and the predictive contribution of earlier patent growth.  

# Data

Coal observations start in 1854 and cannot estimate pre-1852 coal growth. Railway mileage measures annual increases in lines opened, not the total network. The railway source includes Ireland from 1845 through 1912; earlier observations cover Great Britain only.  Patent applications and sealed patents describe separate annual administrative counts and do not identify matched cohorts.
The industrial data are NBER Macrohistory series distributed through FRED: coal production, A01211GBA418NNBR and railway line openings, A02085GBA374NNBR. The patent workbook is from Petra Moser's Pirates and Patents research collection, commit c5c38cc4e59a803fcc8df800a29091eb780dbc98. Its source documentation credits Brian R. Mitchell, British Historical Statistics (1988).
